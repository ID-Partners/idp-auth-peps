Change the world while it runs. Nothing below restarts a PEP or edits its configuration. Re-run after each; the PEPs pick the change up on their next metadata refresh.

The columns are the argument. Same request, same policy, three ways of deciding who decides.
Grey trace lines are metadata fetches the PEP made. Green is the PDP that decided. Orange is the rogue PDP being consulted at all.
Every endpoint here is AuthZEN's own name — /access/v1/evaluation and /access/v1/evaluations. Nothing invents a path. What differs is the base they hang off: a PDP identifier may carry one, and /tenants/bank-a is what a multi-tenant deployment looks like.
The PEP judged none of it. Each resource publishes what it requires — scopes_supported (RFC 9728) and an example acr_values_required — and the PEP forwards that document verbatim, with the endpoint hit and (when the route allows) the raw token. The was given line is what the PDP had to work with. Matching a token to a requirement is the PDP's decision, next to everything else it knows; a gateway comparing strings would be policy in two places, and the dumber one would win.
So the badge is about the base. A PEP that read no metadata posts under the PDP's own identifier path. Relocate the PDP and only the PEPs that read metadata follow — which is the whole argument, and it is invisible until something moves.
Metadata caches are set to a short TTL here so the trace stays visible on every run; the shipped default is five minutes.
