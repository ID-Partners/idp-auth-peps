Change the world while it runs. Nothing below restarts a PEP or edits its configuration. Re-run after each; the PEPs pick the change up on their next metadata refresh.

The columns are the argument. Same request, same policy, three ways of deciding who decides.
Grey trace lines are metadata fetches the PEP made. Green is the PDP that decided. Orange is the rogue PDP being consulted at all.
Watch the endpoint badge. /access/v1/evaluation is the path AuthZEN says to assume when a PDP publishes no metadata. Anything else is the endpoint that PDP advertised, and a PEP only knows it by having read the document.
Metadata caches are set to a short TTL here so the trace stays visible on every run; the shipped default is five minutes.
